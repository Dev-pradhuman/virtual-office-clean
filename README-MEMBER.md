# 👥 Virtual Office - Teammate Member Guide

Welcome to the Virtual Office! This guide helps **Teammates & Members** install, connect, and collaborate within the application.

---

## 1. Quick Start Installation

### For Windows Users:
1. Download `Virtual Office Setup 1.0.0.exe` from your team coordinator.
2. Run the installer setup to install the application.
3. Launch **Virtual Office** from your Desktop or Start Menu.

### For Linux / Ubuntu Users:
1. Unpack the `virtual-office-1.0.0.tar.gz` archive:
   ```bash
   tar -xzf virtual-office-1.0.0.tar.gz
   ```
2. Navigate to the folder and make the binary executable:
   ```bash
   cd linux-unpacked
   chmod +x "Virtual Office"
   ```
3. Run the application:
   ```bash
   ./"Virtual Office"
   ```

---

## 2. Dynamic Team Presence
- **Automated Room Backdrops:** The office workspace wallpaper updates dynamically depending on who is currently online, visually representing active team states.
- **Auto-Minimize to Tray:** Clicking the **X** close button preserves active presence and monitors teammate notifications in the system tray. Restore or quit the app by right-clicking the tray icon.

---

## 3. Account Customization & Avatar Uploads
Navigate to the **Settings** tab in the sidebar to configure your workstation identity:
- **Designation:** Set your custom professional title (e.g. *Lead Designer*, *Senior Developer*).
- **Password:** Change your account login password.
- **Avatar PFP:** Click **Upload local image** to load any JPEG/PNG file under 2MB directly from your local filesystem.
- **Aesthetic Colors:** Change your personal workstation theme accent (Sunset, Cyberpunk, Rose, Forest, etc.) to customize glows, lamps, and monitors.
- **3D Office Toggle:** Toggle the **Enable 3D Office Scene (WebGL)** checkbox to switch between the 2D wallpaper mapping and the live 3D Three.js scene.

---

## 4. Interacting with Edith (AI Assistant)
Click **🤖 Edith AI** in the sidebar navigation or click the floating bubble in the corner:
- **Smart Summaries:** Ask Edith *"summarize activity"* to see a detailed, conversational description of what you and your teammates are working on based on open applications.
- **Telemetry Queries:** Type or speak queries like *"who is online"* or *"what apps are open"*.
- **High-Quality Speech:** Edith reads back responses dynamically using high-fidelity online/neural female voice synthesis.
- **Voice Recognition:** Click the microphone button and dictate commands directly.
